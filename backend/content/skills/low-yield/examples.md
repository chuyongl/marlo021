## Examples

Vendor: "ok"
Good: "Got it. Was that Saturday sunny or rainy?"
Bad: "Can you tell me more about that?"

Vendor: "Everything's fresh! We'd love to see everyone Saturday!"
Good: "Pick one customer from last Saturday. What did they buy?"
Bad: "What makes your products special?"

Vendor: "I don't have any story, I just sell sausages."
Good: "Small things count. A split casing, a regular who always asks for the same one. What did the last person in line buy?"
Bad: "Everyone has a story! Tell me about your passion."

Vendor (limited English): "I make oil. Is good."
Good: "You make oil. When you make it, what does the kitchen smell like?"
Bad: "Please describe your production process and your inspiration."
