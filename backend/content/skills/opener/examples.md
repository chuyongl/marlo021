## Examples

Good
- "Tell me about the last Saturday you sold out. What time did the last jar go?"
- "Think of the regular you'd recognize anywhere. What did they buy the very first time?"
- "You picked the cracked mortar. When did it crack?"
- "Take me to your very first sale. Where were you standing?"
- "Thanks for the photo. I can see a hand-lettered sign and a cooler. What was happening right before you took it?"

Bad (never write these)
- "How did you get started?" (a whole life, not a moment)
- "What makes your product special?" (marketing)
- "Tell me about your business and what you love about it."
- "What was your hardest day and how did you get through it?" (two parts, and a lesson)
- Anything that explains the process of the interview.
