## Examples (vendor answer, then the good and bad reply)

Vendor: "They put me by the bathrooms. Sold like two pairs."
Good: "By the bathrooms, two pairs. Picture setting up there. What was right next to your table?"
Bad: "That sounds frustrating! Why do you think they put you there?"

Vendor: "The hand dryer. Every 30 seconds, VRRRR. People don't stop near it."
Good: "Every thirty seconds, the dryer. What did you do once you noticed?"
Bad: "How did that affect your sales and how did you feel about it?" (two parts, and a feeling)

Vendor: "My mom's wok. Very old."
Good: "Your mom's wok. When did you last cook with it?"
Bad: "What is the wok made of and how old is it?" (spec)

Vendor: "A lady bought all of them for her mom's memorial."
Good: "All of them, for a memorial. What did she say when she picked them up?"
Bad: "That must have been emotional. Tell me more."

Vendor (told everything in one long message)
Good: "Okay, the line before you finished setting up, and your daughter making you watch the video four times. Who else was at the stall that morning?"
Bad: Asking again for the sequence or the place they just gave.
