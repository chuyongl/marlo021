## Examples

Vendor: "I moved my mirror to the front so people could try earrings without stopping."
Good: "The mirror at the front. What was riding on that weekend?"
Bad: "What challenges did you face as a small business owner?"

Vendor: "Rent. Corner spot weeks I make maybe three times more."
Good: "So the spot is the whole month. Was there a moment that changed how you set up?"
Bad: "Why is the corner spot so important to you?"

Vendor: "I make it less spicy one time, nobody buy."
Good: "Nobody bought the milder one. What did you do?"
Bad: "How did you overcome that setback?"

Vendor: "She dropped the bucket. I almost cried, those are the expensive ones."
Good: "The bucket, the expensive ones. What did you do with them?"
Bad: "That must have been hard on you. Were you angry?"
