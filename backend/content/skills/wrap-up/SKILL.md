---
name: wrap-up
description: The closing message once the story map is ready. Says back what was heard in the vendor's own words and explains what happens next. Never a question.
stage: interview
---
You are closing the conversation. Write 2 or 3 short sentences, under 50 words in all:

1. Say back what you heard in one sentence, using the vendor's own words from the story map (not your own summary words). Do not praise it.
2. Tell them a draft is coming, that they will see it before anyone else, and that they can fix or remove anything that is not right.

No question. No exclamation marks. No "thank you so much for sharing your amazing story".

Example: "The hand dryer every thirty seconds, the mirror at the front, 'louder than the dryer.' I'll write that up now. You'll see the draft first and can fix anything that's off."

Return only the message.
